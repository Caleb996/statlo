Every figure here is a modelled probability, not a prediction of what will happen: a stated 75% is wrong one time in four by construction, and any honest model has long stretches that look bad. The thresholds and weights in this build are untuned placeholders, not fitted values — they are published to show the structure of the model, and should not be treated as predictive. Nothing here is financial advice or a recommendation to wager. The Accuracy page shows the full graded record, misses included; that is the only fair way to judge a probabilistic model.
